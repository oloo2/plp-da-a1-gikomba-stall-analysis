## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. Over the past two weeks, she has manually tracked daily sales and inventory stock levels in a notebook. To assess her business health, track growth trends, optimize inventory restocking, and identify sales patterns across weekdays and weekends, this analysis evaluates her 14-day performance using core Python to deliver actionable, data-driven business decisions.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Strong Weekend Sales Surge:** Weekend daily sales average **KES 4,900.00**, generating **82.50% higher revenue** than weekday sales (**KES 2,685.00**). Saturday of Week 2 registered peak performance at **KES 5,600.00**.
2. **Positive Week-on-Week Growth:** Business revenue grew by **10.66%**, rising from **KES 22,050.00** in Week 1 to **KES 24,400.00** in Week 2.
3. **Low Inventory Risk for Key Items:** **Tomatoes** (18 kg) and **Sukuma** (9 kg) are critically below their reorder levels of 25 kg and 15 kg, respectively, risking stockouts if not replenished before the weekend.
